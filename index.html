Dr.-Ing. Sarah Ouerghemmi

Hi, my name is Sarah and I am

  • RF and embedded hardware engineer, Dr.-Ing. with highest distinction
  • Associate Researcher at HTWK Leipzig, in the Smart Diagnostics and Online Monitoring group
  • Doctoral graduate of HTWK Leipzig and ENET'COM Sfax, under joint supervision
  • Member of the IEEE

I spend my days making radios listen for less power. Over the past five years I have built ultra-low-power RF front ends and low-noise amplifiers for wake-up receivers, taking them from circuit concept through ADS simulation and PCB layout to a prototype measured on the bench. Alongside the hardware I publish in international journals and conferences, give seminars, and supervise graduation projects.

What I care about is the distance between a result on a bench and a product that uses it. I am currently looking for an industrial R&D or project leadership role where I can close it.
